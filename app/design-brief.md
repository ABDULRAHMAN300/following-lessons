# Following Lessons design brief

## Design read
A private Arabic-first planning space for one science-content designer who needs calm control, visible momentum, and continuity across devices.

## Concept spine
The interface is a living scientific orbit: subjects are stable bodies, lessons are satellites, and completion brings each item into a clear settled state.

## Delivery
Cinema opening with a controlled scroll-scrub film; the working dashboard stays fast, tactile, and legible.

## Locked identity
Palette: deep laboratory green #113B36, cool mist #EDF5F1, soft white #F8FBF9, ink #122421, muted ink #58706B, single watermelon coral accent #E76572.
Type: IBM Plex Sans Arabic with IBM Plex Mono for numerals and compact metadata.
Hero: cinematic scientific orbit. Dashboard: asymmetric masthead, radial progress, sliced subject rail, divided lesson rows. Corners stay mostly square; circles belong to orbit and completion states.

Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey block
Journey shape: single-shot.
1. Orbit: floating glass spheres establish chemistry, physics, and integrated science.
2. Selection: the camera approaches a subject sphere while grade paths resolve.
3. Progress: lesson satellites shift from open outlines to coral completion rings.
4. Continuity: synchronized light pulses travel between device-like planes.
5. Resolve: the orbit settles into a clean center-safe frame.
World grammar: translucent scientific glass, deep laboratory green, coral completion pulses, cool mist reflections, steady upper-left key light, restrained bloom, no on-screen text.
Mobile framing keeps focal objects in the center 55 percent with a lighter vertical encode.
Delivery budget: desktop at or below 32 MiB; mobile at or below 16 MiB.

## Product structure
1. Animated welcome with one enter action.
2. Owner setup or sign-in before lesson data.
3. Dashboard with completion summary and three subject groups.
4. Grade selector: Chemistry and Physics 10, 11, 12; Integrated Science 10.
5. Lesson add, rename, delete, and one-press completion.
6. Search and completion filter.
7. Sign-out and sync status.

## Asset plan
Generated molecular cover scene, technical dot and glass plates, empty-state illustration, desktop and mobile film encodes, exact first-frame posters, orbital favicon, launch cover, and social image. Functional controls use one Phosphor icon family.

## Functional contract
Arabic RTL interface. One owner account is created on first use, then registration locks. Passwords are salted and hashed; authentication uses secure HttpOnly sessions. D1 stores the owner, sessions, and unlimited user-named lessons for cross-device synchronization. Every write is server-authorized and owner-scoped. Completion, rename, and deletion persist immediately. Full keyboard focus, strong contrast, responsive layout, and reduced-motion fallback are required. No community-feed publication.

## Build constraint note
Paid video generation was unavailable on the connected plan. The scroll-controlled journey therefore uses a deterministic 15-second cinematic camera move encoded from the generated molecular scene. This preserves the animated interaction and living-system concept without stock imagery.
